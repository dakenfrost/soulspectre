Kharos Infrastructure

The Forges of the Dominion
The Kharos Dominion builds with ruthless efficiency. Their infrastructure is a monument to endless war and dark bargains, ranging from brutal combat arenas to unholy altars and demonic libraries. Every structure serves to upgrade the endless tide of warriors, undead, cultists, and mercenaries that march under their banner.
