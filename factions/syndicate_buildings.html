Syndicate Infrastructure

Offices, Manors & Infernal Foundries
The Devil Syndicate builds the way it does business: with precision, paperwork and a price for everything. Its capitals combine the gilded manors of the Infernal Courts with the offices, foundries and archives of the corporate wing, and every structure exists for one purpose: to turn raw recruits, bound demons and ambitious operatives into assets worth investing in.
