The Unbroken Chain

The Iron Brotherhood is a lawful collective of dwarves in which individual ambition is considered a flaw. Every citizen is a link in a great chain that binds the whole society together, and every dwarf calls the others "brother".

Tactical Doctrine

On the battlefield the Brotherhood is slow, stubborn and extremely hard to break. Its warriors endure what would crush other armies, while craftsmen and shamans prepare the moment when the whole chain strikes as one.
