Coalition Spells

The Language of the Great Cycle
The spells of the Primal Coalition are not cast , they are called. They are not commands issued to a reluctant world, but agreements struck with forces that were already moving. A Coalition caster does not impose fire; she asks it to go somewhere specific. She does not summon roots; she invites them to remember what was buried. These spells are conversations with the Great Cycle, and like all conversations worth having, they require the speaker to mean what they say.

Level 5 Spells
2400 World Tremor A catastrophic seismic event strikes a target territory. All enemy units suffer heavy Earth damage and are Stunned for 1 turn. All allied units in adjacent territories gain +25% ATK for 2 turns from the resonant energy.
2400 The Cycle Devours All enemy units in the target territory lose 15% of their maximum HP per turn for 3 turns as the land itself feeds on them. Each HP consumed restores 5% HP to a random allied unit in the same territory.
2400 Khora's Ascension Khora temporarily manifests in a target territory she is not present in, dealing full Fire + Earth AoE damage and applying 2 Smolder stacks to all enemies. Duration: 1 turn. Cooldown: once per battle.
2400 Primal Sovereignty The Primal Coalition asserts dominion. For 3 turns, no enemy force may move through or occupy any territory adjacent to the Coalition's capital without suffering immediate 30% max HP damage. Enemy spells cast toward Coalition territories have a 40% chance to fail.
