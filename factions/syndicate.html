Devil Syndicate

Corporate efficiency meets abyssal corruption.
"Hypocrisy is the homage which vice pays to virtue." — La Rochefoucauld, Massime

The Business of Souls

The Devil Syndicate is a society as old as humanity itself, operating from the depths of Hell. Their primary objective is the subtle corruption of mortals, either to recruit them into their ranks or to acquire their souls. Despite their inherently evil and selfish nature, these devils disdain mindless violence, brutality, and death, considering them merely as "wasted resources" in the grand ledger of existence.

Their culture is deeply fractured. On one side stands the Ancient Nobility, clinging to a feudal power structure where Dukes and Barons rule over decadent abyssal courts. On the other side is Capitalist Innovation, heavily inspired by human corporate models, which believes that merit, productivity, and measurable results are the only true metrics of power.

Executive Doctrine

Beneath the seething tensions between the old aristocracy and the new capitalists, the Devil Syndicate is governed with an iron fist by Chief Executive Officers (CEOs). Handpicked by the enigmatic "Abyssal President", these executives manage specific territories and high-stakes missions. In this infernal bureaucracy, efficiency is paramount, and everyone knows the golden rule: those who break the system's order are swiftly and permanently liquidated.
