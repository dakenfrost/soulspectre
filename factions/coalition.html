The Ancient Heartbeat

The Primal Coalition answers an ancient call: the heartbeat of the planet itself. They are not mindless monsters, but the antibodies of the world , a symbiotic alliance born from the fusion of the ruthless efficiency of animal predators, the unstoppable resilience of flora, and the purifying fury of the elements.

They live by a sacred law of harmony and natural selection, yet they have understood that to protect their ancestral domain, they must evolve beyond the feral state. Through exposure to ancient veins of planetary energy, they have developed living armor of bio-obsidian and a lethal tactical intelligence forged in the oldest fires of the world.

Tactical Doctrine

The Coalition does not fight battles , it hunts. Their forces blend the explosive ferocity of beast-kin with the patient, rooted endurance of the living forest. Felid warriors strike faster than thought; Spore-wielding druids dissolve formation cohesion from within. Every engagement is an ecosystem: predator and flora working as one, consuming the enemy like a fire consumes dry wood , leaving only stronger ground behind.
