Coalition Infrastructure

Grown from Stone, Shaped by Fire
The Coalition does not build , it cultivates and erupts. Their structures are either coaxed from the living forest over seasons of patient growth, or forged in a single volcanic surge of pressurized mineral energy. An Apex Den is grown by warriors who sleep alongside the roots for weeks, their bio-obsidian resonating with the soil until the structure forms around them. An Echo Spire is raised in one night by a ring of druids who channel planetary energy upward through connected root systems. To inhabit one is to be part of the planet's own immune response.
