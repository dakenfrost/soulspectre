The CEO

The Will of the Abyssal President

Chosen by the President

Chief Executive Officers are appointed directly by Lucifer, the Abyssal President, and answer to no one else. Each of them receives a single mandate: a mission the President considers too important to leave to the ordinary machinery of Hell. It may be the conquest of a kingdom, the defense of an infernal province, the slow corruption of a nation or a delicate negotiation with another power.

The President never tells a CEO how to succeed. He only defines what success looks like, and expects it to be delivered. A CEO who fulfills their mandate is celebrated across Hell and trusted with ever greater missions; one who fails rarely receives a second appointment.

Every Resource of Hell

To fulfill their mandate, a CEO may requisition whatever they need, and no one in Hell can refuse them:

  • The Infernal Courts – Noble armies, ancient sorcery and centuries of military experience. Even a Duke must lend his forces, however much it wounds his pride.
  • The Corporate Wing – Weapons from the foundries, agents from the intelligence divisions, archives, contracts and the bureaucratic machinery that keeps Hell running.
  • The Abyss – Through the Contractors, the raw power of bound demons, from expendable Imps to the most expensive contracts the Syndicate has ever signed.

The Ideal Devil

Cold, calculating and magnetic, CEOs are the apotheosis of what a devil is meant to become. They weigh every soldier, every soul and every risk on the same scale, and they never let pride, honor or cruelty distort the numbers. Some win their wars with a handshake and a well-worded contract; others prefer subtle schemes and quiet sabotage. All of them deliver.

A CEO is rarely a formidable fighter in the physical sense, and has no need to be. Their command of legendary infernal magic can sweep entire armies from the battlefield, and their mere presence is enough to make lesser beings forget why they came to fight.

Explosive Eruption

Base Skill

The CEO tears open the ground beneath the enemy lines, releasing a surge of hellfire.

  • Deals 100% of attack as damage to all enemies in a group.

Abyssal Annihilation

Active Skill

The sky splits open as the CEO calls down a rain of burning stone from the Abyss.

  • Throws 5 meteors at random enemies.
  • Each meteor deals 100% of attack as Physical damage and 100% of attack as Fire damage.

Ambition of the Conqueror

Passive

Nothing can bend the will of a devil who has already decided to win.

  • Immune to control effects (stun, fear, mind control), unless immunity is negated or bypassed.
  • Gains a Shield equal to 100% of attack at the start of their turn.
  • At the end of their turn, 10% chance to apply the Mind debuff Fear to all enemies.

Soul Shatter

Ultimate

The CEO reaches into the target's very essence and calls in the debt.

  • Select a target. If it is not immune to Soul and below Tier 6, it is killed instantly: it cannot be resurrected and is removed from the battle.
  • Otherwise, deals 150% of attack as Physical damage and applies the Physical debuff Stun for 1 turn.
⚠ Warning: Should the CEO fall, the Devil Syndicate is defeated, and the player immediately loses the game.
