Eclipse Ravens

Ranged Executions & Black Ops

The Shadows of the Boardroom

The Eclipse Ravens represent the Syndicate's premier ranged executioners and black-ops division. Unlike the frontline brawlers of the Black Guards, the Ravens pride themselves on ruthless efficiency, modern ballistics, and tactical shadow traversal to dismantle enemy lines before a formal engagement even begins.

Each operational cell is directed by a highly analytical Commander, an officer who orchestrates the flow of battle, shielding critical assets while calling in devastating demonic artillery. Standard operatives begin their careers as Troopers, armed with corporate-issue crossbows.

From there, they specialize. Traditionalists refine their accuracy to become Archers, Magic Archers, and armor-piercing Infernal Snipers (Marksmanship Path). Those who embrace the Syndicate's modern tech trade bows for dual firearms, becoming volatile Gunslingers and bullet-hell Hitmen (Ballistics Path). A third, highly secretive sect delves into the void: the Shadows, Shades, and Nightblades (Assassination Path) who rely on evasion, backstabs, and debilitating bio-toxins.

The absolute pinnacle of this division is the Endsinger, a Tier V executioner whose requiem bullets bypass all known physical defenses to bring the final curtain down on the Syndicate's enemies.
