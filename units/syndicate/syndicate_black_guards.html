The Black Guards

Executors of Corporate Will

Frontline Liquidators

The Black Guards are the brutal yet disciplined frontline infantry of the Devil Syndicate. These forces are strictly compartmentalized and often commanded by a Noble Scion—high-ranking aristocrats of established houses who lead their own private armies to expand their corporate portfolios.

New recruits enter the ranks as Soldiers, baseline operatives equipped for standard engagements. Those who survive their initial performance reviews are promoted to Legionaries, where their career path diverges based on their combat metrics.

Operatives who excel in asset protection transfer to the Bulwark Path, becoming impenetrable Hellguards and eventually Dark Knights. Conversely, those who demonstrate a talent for punitive action join the Assault Path as Enforcers, tasked with delivering brutal justice to criminals. The most ruthless and unscrupulous of these become Blood Knights, specialized in executing syndicate enemies without hesitation.

At the apex of this military structure stands the Arbiter. These Tier V Champions possess absolute authority on the battlefield, acting as judge, jury, and executioner.
