House of Duty

Arcane Bureaucracy & Domestic Inquisitions

The Domestic Judiciary

Within the decadent and competitive courts of the Syndicate's feudal nobility, the arcane arts are managed strictly as corporate and domestic assets. The House of Duty encompasses the trusted servants, judges, executioners, and espionage agents who maintain internal order across infernal estates.

Aspirants begin their service as either Pages, specializing in psychological control and dark manipulation, or Servants, focused on logistical support and defensive barriers. As their contracts are renewed and optimized, their roles specialize further.

The control lineage branches into penal and psychological assets like Jailers, Courtesans, Torturers, and Succubi, eventually culminating in merciless Judges and Temptresses. Concurrently, the support lineage advances through efficient Maids, protective Butlers, Head Maids, and Stewards, reaching elite executive tiers as Mistresses and Chamberlains.

At the pinnacle of this magical administration sits the Governor—a Tier V synthesis of control, healing, and destructive wind-dark mastery who governs the estate with absolute authority.
