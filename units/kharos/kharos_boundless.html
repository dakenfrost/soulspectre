The Boundless

Dark Pacts & Primal Forces

The Architects of Chaos

The group that seeks ultimate magical power calls itself The Boundless. Lacking the patience for traditional academic study—which they find slow and impractical—they instead seek shortcuts through pacts and contracts with demons, devils, or volatile nature spirits.

While every initiate starts as a Cultist, they eventually split into divergent paths: those who channel infernal powers to become Warlocks, and those who embrace the raw, savage power of nature as shapeshifting Druids.

Arcanists are the rare few with high academic proficiency. In a society where many lose themselves to dangerous entities, Arcanists act as the "enforcers." For instance, when a Warlock fails and becomes a Possessed, an Arcanist steps in to bind the demon to the Dominion’s will using special markings, turning a potential disaster into a valuable asset.
