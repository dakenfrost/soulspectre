The Outsiders

Mercenaries & Irregular Warfare

The Shadows of the Dominion

The Outsiders are a loose collective of individuals who prefer to work alone or in small, highly specialized cells. This chaotic group is home to all sorts of rogues: mercenaries, thieves, bounty hunters, and even renegade alchemists rejected by traditional societies.

Typically, each cell falls under the jurisdiction of an Overseer, a calculating veteran who monitors their activities, supplies experimental armaments, and ensures that all brutal contracts are fulfilled to the letter. These bands are led into the most challenging and high-stakes missions by agile Rogues, masters of guerrilla warfare.

The standard operative begins as a common Thief or Bandit, using dirty tricks and throwing daggers. As they survive and amass wealth, they either refine their precision to become lethal Mercenaries and Bounty Hunters, or trade their blades for volatile chemicals to serve as devastating Bombers and Alchemists.
