Echoes of the Ancients

The Spirit of the Wild

The Echoes of the Ancients is a faction composed entirely of female and hybrid creatures, each carrying the essence of their animal or monstrous lineage. These beings have united to harness their primal instincts and ancient powers for survival and dominance in the Void.

Bound by their connection to the forces that shaped the world, they view technology as a tool to enhance organic evolution rather than replace it. Their ideology is deeply rooted in the balance between nature and nurture, where raw strength and ancestral knowledge coexist harmoniously.

Their four major branches represent different types of hybridization and habitats: Terrestrial Predators, Aerial Creatures, Aquatic Beasts, and Plant-Symbionts. Each branch has its own unique evolution path, reflecting the diversity of their origins and abilities.
